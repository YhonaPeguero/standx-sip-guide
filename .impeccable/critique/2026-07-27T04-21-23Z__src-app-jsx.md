## Design Specificity Verdict

**One surface of four is authored for this product. The other three are category-interchangeable, and the hero converges on the brief's own declared anti-reference.**

**LLM assessment.** Product-specific and genuinely uncopyable: the `not published` chip (`VaultLabels.jsx`) rendered inline at the same visual weight as real data, nine times across the vaults tab; the liquidation waterfall (`LiquidationFlow.jsx:171-197`) where node activation is *derived* from reader input rather than decorated; the trading gate teaching the AND-relationship the proposal specifies; the verbatim SIP header table as a mono `<dl>`.

Category-interchangeable: the hero (`Hero.jsx`) is eyebrow pill → 84px display headline with a gradient on the second clause → two CTAs → four-item numbered row, over an animated ripple field — the shape of every dark-mode crypto landing page since 2021, and specifically the "purple-gradient AI landing" anti-reference with the hue swapped to green. The entire playbook tab is a 2×2 B2B features grid with four CTAs resolving to two destinations. The chart chrome is standard fintech furniture plotting fabricated targets.

**Deterministic scan.** CLI: `detect.mjs --json src/` → exit 2, **6 findings, all in `src/styles/index.css`**; zero fired in any of the 42 `.jsx` files. `index.html` and `src/App.jsx` scanned clean.

## Overall Impression

The vaults tab is the product. It solves a genuinely hard problem — rendering absence as information — and it does so with a primitive nothing else on the web has. Everything around it is weaker, and the two surfaces a first-time visitor sees first (hero, then simulator) are the two that most undercut the thing the guide is selling: that its numbers can be trusted.

The single biggest opportunity is not visual. It is that **the simulator's numbers currently argue with each other on screen**, in the one product whose entire claim is numeric discipline.

## What's Working

**1. The `not published` chip as a first-class primitive.** A dashed mono chip inline beside the label it disclaims, at the same weight as real data — not small print. A reader seeing `LIQUIDATION FEE [not published]` above an empty input understands both that the field matters and that the proposal is silent. This is Product Principle 1 rendered as UI, and it is the one thing a neighbouring explainer could not copy without doing the same source work.

**2. The liquidation waterfall's derived state.** `active={hasPosition && outcome.absorbedByShield > 0}`, `active={reachesAdl}`. Entering $1,000,000 / $250,000 produces `Absorbed by Shield Vault $250K · Reserved capacity left $0.00 · Reaches ADL $750K`, with ADL switching to `--sx-accent` so the bad outcome reads differently **without a new colour**. Teaches ordering, isolation and exhaustion with zero invented figures.

**3. The spacing and elevation system earns depth from the locked palette.** `--sx-gap-section` (112px) against `--sx-gap-header` (56px) is a deliberate 2:1 ratio applied through two utilities on every tab. Every shadow opens with `inset 0 1px 0 rgba(242,248,244,…)`, so surfaces read as lit rather than outlined — depth without a new hue, exactly the "more life from existing colors" the brief asked for. Measurement backs it: `--sx-text` 18.68:1, `--sx-muted` 7.91:1, `--sx-primary-bright` 7.46:1 on `--sx-bg`. Only one token pair fails, and `prefers-reduced-motion` is honoured properly in `Reveal.jsx`, `Hero.jsx`, and `.sx-ring`.

## Priority Issues

### [P0] The simulator's numbers contradict each other, and the headline figure is mislabelled
**What.** `ValueDisplay.jsx:19` renders `estimatedValue` under the label `Total Yield Generated` (`en.js:530`) — at default state, **$10,262.26** for $10,000 of capital, when the yield generated is $262.26. Directly below, `ProtocolStats.jsx:33` hardcodes `value={isOn ? '+' + gainLabel : '$0.00'}`, so `ESTIMATED GAIN` reads **$0.00** while `YIELD %` reads **2.62%** on the same row, and the Scenario Comparison 40px lower correctly prints `+$262.26 (2.62%)`. Three mutually inconsistent readings in one 816px row. `calculateSimulationSnapshot` computes `estimatedGain` correctly; the display throws it away.
**Why it matters.** The product's whole claim to trust is numeric discipline. A reader who notices Gain $0.00 beside Yield 2.62% has no reason to believe anything else — including the vaults tab, where the discipline is real and hard-won. Present in all five locales.
**Fix.** Change `valueDisplay.label` to `Estimated Value` (five strings), or pass `simulated.estimatedGain` if the label stays. Delete the `isOn ?` branch at `ProtocolStats.jsx:33` and always render the gain; let `valueColor` — which the component already animates — carry the off-state signal.
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable clarify`

### [P0] The guide overlay is a keyboard trap
**What.** `GuideOverlay.jsx` renders no `role="dialog"` (verified: count 0), never moves focus on open (`document.activeElement` is `BODY` after Start Guide), has no focus trap and no Escape handler (verified: still open after Escape). Tabbing lands on the dimmed content *behind* it — measured order: four `Read more→`, `View sub-proposals+`, then three footer links. The guide's own Back/Next/Skip are unreachable until the reader tabs the entire overview. Dimming layers are `pointer-events-auto`, so the page is click-blocked while staying tab-reachable.
**Why it matters.** This is the designated first-visit path, offered automatically to everyone. A keyboard or screen-reader user who accepts is put into a state with no visible focus, no announcement, no Escape, and no accessible route to the two controls that advance or exit. Note the contrast with the rest of the app, which measures clean: 0 images missing alt, 0 unlabeled controls, 31/31 tabbable elements reachable on the simulator.
**Fix.** Add `role="dialog" aria-modal="true" aria-labelledby={stepTitleId}` to the `motion.section` at `GuideOverlay.jsx:133`; focus the panel on open; restore focus to Guide Mode on close; add an Escape listener calling `onSkip`; mark `#main-content` `inert` while open.
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

### [P1] Nested cards — the declared anti-reference — on three of four surfaces
**What.** Detected in-page and confirmed by direct DOM inspection: 4 on overview, 3 on simulator, 3 on vaults. A bordered, filled, rounded, shadowed surface inside another bordered, filled, rounded surface — `div.border.p-5` (1px `rgba(0,102,50,0.55)`, radius 6px, own box-shadow, 20px padding) inside `article.border.p-5` (1px `rgb(26,42,37)`, radius 6px, 28px padding). Also `ul.divide-y.border` inside `article.p-6.border`.
**Why it matters.** This is one of the three anti-references named in the brief, and it is the reason the denser surfaces read as busy despite the disciplined spacing system. The CLI scan cannot see it — the nesting is composed at runtime across components — so it will not appear in any static check.
**Fix.** Pick one carrier per nesting level. Inner surfaces become plain regions: keep the padding and the `divide-y`, drop border, background and radius. Depth is already available from the inset-highlight shadow ladder without a second frame.
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable distill`

### [P1] Three focus treatments, one of which fails WCAG 2.2 contrast
**What.** Two problems that compound. Semantically, `--sx-accent` pink is simultaneously focus (`--sx-shadow-focus`, `index.css:80`), invalid input (`CapitalSimulator.jsx`), ADL danger (`LiquidationFlow.jsx:216`), and the chart endpoint dot — so a focused, perfectly valid liquidation input is ringed in the same pink as a rejected capital field. Mechanically, a third un-themed system has leaked through: Tailwind's default `rgba(59,130,246,0.5)` blue ring with a fully transparent outline, measuring **2.13:1** on `--sx-bg` — below the WCAG 2.2 non-text minimum of 3:1 — at only **1px** spread on the five timeframe buttons, 2px on Guide Mode, the language menu and the 24×24 tooltip button. The designed pink ring measures 6.74:1 and is visible; the leaked blue one is not.
**Why it matters.** A reader cannot distinguish focused from rejected, and keyboard users on the leaked controls get an indicator that fails the standard outright. Pink is the only non-green hue in the system, so spending it on focus leaves nothing for errors.
**Fix.** Reserve `--sx-accent` for error and danger only. Build the focus ring from the existing greens — `0 0 0 2px var(--sx-bg), 0 0 0 4px var(--sx-primary-glow)` is brighter and higher-contrast than the pink and reads as the brand. Then delete the leaked Tailwind ring so one system remains. No new hue: this is precisely the "more life from the existing colors" the brief asked for.
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable colorize`

### [P1] The simulator's main column ends 539px above its own sidebar
**What.** Measured at 1440×1000: the left `<article>` is 816×722 ending at y=1252; the `<aside>` runs to y=1791, both stretched by `lg:grid-cols-[minmax(0,1fr)_340px]` (`SimulatorView.jsx:60`). That is an **816×539px empty rectangle** in the primary content column — roughly 23% of the 2330px page. Turning SIP #2 on still leaves ~450px.
**Why it matters.** It reads as a page that failed to load, and it forces the SIP #2 toggle (sidebar, y≈900–1100) apart from the stats it changes (left column, y≈1250). The one surface whose job is showing a difference makes the reader look elsewhere to see it.
**Fix.** `self-start` on the `<aside>` lets the row collapse to natural height. Better: move the two `ScenarioComparison` cards into the vacated space as a full-width row beneath the grid, putting the OFF/ON comparison at the same eye level as the toggle driving it.
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable layout`

### [P2] Mobile — sub-44px touch targets throughout, and the nav label collides in two locales
**What.** Targets under 44×44 per view: overview 24, simulator 28, playbook 18, vaults 21 — **all failing on height, not width**. Worst: the 24×24 scenario tooltip, the 44×24 "Turn yield on" switch, 25.5px-tall scenario buttons, 34px timeframe buttons, and all nine footer links at 19.5px. Separately, at 390px the BottomNav cell is 98px while the playbook label measures 98px (pt-BR "Playbook de Yield") and 100px (uk "Плейбук прибутковості"); both wrap with `leading-none` so the lines collide and the uk label runs into the Vaults column.
**Why it matters.** PRODUCT.md Principle 4 says layout must survive the longest locale, and this is the persistent navigation on the platform most shared links open on — broken in the two locales already carrying `// TODO: needs native review`.
**Fix.** Raise the interactive minimum to 44px height via the shared control tokens rather than per-element. For the nav, add a short-label key per locale (`Playbook` / `Плейбук` / `플레이북`) — five strings, one line everywhere. Don't just reduce type size; that postpones the collision.
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable adapt`

## Minor Observations

- **The simulator's illustrative figures are marked, but the marking is subordinate to the number.** `+121.00%` renders at 58px in bright green beside a pulsing ACTIVE dot, derived from `target: 2210.0` in `src/constants/chart.js:56` alongside `poolLabel: '22 Pools'` and `rebalanceLabel: '23m 33s'`. The caveat *does* exist — `Illustrative only — the protocol sets the actual rate.` — but at 11px in `--sx-muted-soft`, in a different column. Compare the vaults tab, where `not published` sits inline at data weight. The rule is being followed; the visual hierarchy inverts it.
- `formatCurrencyAdaptive` collapses $999,999 and $1,000,000 to the identical `$1M`; the liquidation grid prints `$250K` beside `$5,000.00` in one 2×2 group.
- `App.jsx:224` rejects the keystroke rather than flagging the value, so `9999999` becomes `999999` with no message; `handleCapitalInputBlur` (`App.jsx:256-260`) then reverts *and* clears the error together.
- The trading gate has no reset — four clicks in, the only way back is four more.
- `ToggleSwitch.jsx` carries two labels for one bit (OFF/IDLE, ON/ACTIVE) on a 122×170px **power-button** glyph, for what the copy calls "an optional layer".
- `SIP #5` shows a pink `WIP` badge on overview while the vaults eyebrow reads `SIP #5B · IMPLEMENTED`. Both true; a badge-scanner sees a contradiction.
- The vaults meta `<dl>` lets `PARENT` wrap to two lines while its five siblings stay on one, so value baselines don't align.
- `--sx-muted-soft` (#778680) measures 4.69:1 on `--sx-surface-2` — clears AA by 0.19 — and is used at 10px and 10.5px, the smallest sizes in the system. It is the floor, not a margin.
- Two checkbox languages: a custom 20px box in the trading gate, a native `<input type="checkbox">` in the guide (`GuideOverlay.jsx:171`).
- `gpt-thin-border-wide-shadow` fired 31 times, always 1px border + 28px blur — one token decision (`--sx-shadow-lg`) amplified across every card, not 31 separate mistakes.
- `em-dash-overuse`: 17 em dashes on overview, 9 on simulator. Worth a pass given the sober-voice brief.
- Overview's Block Options section (~1,000px desktop) is **longer than the entire playbook tab**, which raises the question of why it lives on overview rather than being the fifth surface.

## Questions to Consider

1. **The `not published` chip is the best idea in this product and it exists on one tab.** What would the simulator look like built the same way — a reader-typed rate, `not published` on the rate the protocol sets, and no `target: 2210.0` in `constants/chart.js` at all? Would the reader lose anything they needed, or only lose a number nobody can source?
2. **If the simulator's job is showing the difference SIP #2 makes, why is the default a nearly flat line in 93% empty space with the toggle 539px from the numbers it changes?** What if both OFF and ON were drawn simultaneously and the toggle only changed emphasis — so the comparison *is* the chart?
3. **The hero is an eyebrow pill, a gradient half-headline, a ripple field and two CTAs — the brief's own anti-reference with the hue swapped.** What does the *sober* version of a hero look like for a document explainer? Is the answer "the SIP header field table, at full size, as the first thing on the page"?
4. **Four tabs, but the guide covers three, and the playbook has no numbers and no interaction.** What if the playbook's four cards became four *states the simulator can be put into*, so "Hold DUSD" and "Combine the layers" are chart configurations rather than cards with buttons that navigate away?
5. **Every surface is deep-linkable except the one where reader input exists.** If `#/en/simulator?c=25000&r=1y&sip2=1` were shareable, does the simulator become the thing community members paste into channels — and does that change which surface deserves the most design investment?
6. **On mobile the header is the bare StandX logo.** Given that PRODUCT.md calls independence "a fact to preserve, not a disclaimer to minimize", what would it look like to make unaffiliation the *loudest* thing in the header rather than the quietest thing in a footer 7,000px away?
